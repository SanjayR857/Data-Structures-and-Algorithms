# Learning Roadmap

## Objective
Master Data Structures and Algorithms for FAANG/tier-1 technical interviews and competitive programming across Python, C++, and Java.

---

## Roadmap Phases

### Phase 1: Foundations & Asymptotics (Weeks 1 - 2)
- **Goal:** Understand asymptotic notations ($O, \Omega, \Theta$), recurrence relations, recursion trees, and master theorem.
- **Key Deliverables:** Complete cheat sheets for complexity and master theorem derivations.

### Phase 2: Linear Data Structures & Basic Algorithms (Weeks 3 - 6)
- **Topics:** Dynamic Arrays, Strings, Linked Lists, Stacks, Queues, Sliding Window, Two Pointers.
- **Algorithms:** Binary Search variations, Insertion Sort, Merge Sort, Quick Sort.
- **Problem Target:** 50 problems across LeetCode Easy/Medium.

### Phase 3: Non-Linear Data Structures & Trees (Weeks 7 - 10)
- **Topics:** Binary Trees, BSTs, Heaps/Priority Queues, Tries, Hash Maps & Set collisions.
- **Algorithms:** Tree traversals (Inorder, Preorder, Postorder, Level-order, Morris traversal).
- **Problem Target:** 40 Medium problems.

### Phase 4: Graphs & Advanced Traversal (Weeks 11 - 14)
- **Topics:** Adjacency List/Matrix, BFS, DFS, Topological Sort, Kahn's Algorithm.
- **Advanced:** Dijkstra, Bellman-Ford, Floyd-Warshall, Kruskal, Prim, Disjoint Set Union (DSU).
- **Problem Target:** 35 Medium/Hard problems.

### Phase 5: Dynamic Programming & Backtracking (Weeks 15 - 18)
- **Topics:** Recursion to Memoization to Tabulation; 1D DP, Knapsack 0/1 & Unbounded, LCS, LIS, Interval DP, Bitmask DP.
- **Problem Target:** 45 Medium/Hard problems.

### Phase 6: Interview Drills & Mock Rounds (Weeks 19 - 22)
- **Lists:** Blind 75, NeetCode 150, Company specific drills.
- **Mode:** Timed 45-minute sessions without IDE autocompletion.
